Six Nights at the Story Table

The Story Table is a tabletop roleplaying show for Twitch. Players act from their phones, an AI storyteller plans and narrates each round for the director to approve, and dice decide how it goes. Kevin Huff brought a first version to Claude Code on Thursday night, October 22. By 11:35 AM the next Wednesday, the day of its first live show, nine releases had gone live.

  1. 4:26 AM

    The last live check of the dice build forces double ones to test the new critical failures. The AI's ruling: "Dash is caught red-handed in the manager's office, drawer open, as the break ends."

  2. 4:31 AM

    The check fails. The stream page played the first narration clip to its end, but its one report that the clip had finished never reached the server, so the narration sat there for more than five minutes.

  3. 4:42 AM

    Claude commits the fix. The stream page now repeats that report every two seconds until the server moves on, and the new tests fail on the old code.

  4. 4:55 AM

    Live, 24 minutes after the failure.

    The voices live check plays a full round of narration through on the stream page.

Loot

On the table now

Everything here shipped during the six nights, on top of the first version.

  • A guided workspace for the director and a simple phone seat for each player
  • Producers who help run the show, with their own stream links
  • OBS stream pages: a full scene and a transparent overlay
  • Players write their own characters, and one prompt drafts a whole character or scenario
  • Sogni pictures of scenes, action rounds and beats, plus character portraits
  • A public gallery of each show's pictures
  • Help built into the app
  • A simpler setup and live show for first-timers
  • The Story Table look on every screen and stream page
  • Speak and act in the same round, with lines voiced and the story on the seat
  • Every line rolls, and double ones are a critical failure
  • ElevenLabs voices for players and characters, Eleven v4 emotion tags, and Help me say it

How we counted

  • Times are US Central.
  • Cost is what the tokens in Claude Code's logs for this project would cost at API list prices: every main session, helper agent and sprint agent from October 22 to the afternoon of October 28.
  • Not counted: building the first version, before October 22, and the AI, voice and picture calls the app itself makes.
  • Lines come from git, from the first commit to the ninth release (b99e916). 40,270 of the 55,925 added lines are tests.
  • Words typed covers every message Kevin typed into this project's sessions, side chats included, plus the 12 notes he added to multiple-choice answers. One pasted message from a player (216 words) is left out. A click on an answer counts as an answer, not as words.
  • A sprint run's bar goes from its first agent's start to its last agent's finish. The design language run's 12 hours include an internet outage.
  • Built with Claude Code on Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5.
